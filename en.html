Privacy Policy

Dazzling Cam Shot app · effective October 30, 2026

In short

Your photos are processed and stored on your device only and are never uploaded. The app shows ads using the Yandex advertising SDK, which receives technical, non-identifying device data (see "Ads" below).

Camera

Camera access is used only to take photos inside the app. The camera image is processed on the device and is never sent anywhere.

Photos

  • Photos you take or import are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Photos are copied to your phone gallery only when you ask ("Save to device") or if you turn on auto-save in settings.
  • When importing, the app reads only the photos you pick in the system photo picker.
  • The "Send" button passes a photo to the app you choose (messenger, e-mail, etc.) using standard Android sharing — Dazzling Cam Shot itself is not involved.

Ads

The free version of the app shows banner ads and, from time to time, a full-screen ad after you save photos to your phone gallery. Ads are served by the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (Yandex LLC). To select and show ads, the SDK may collect and send to Yandex:

  • the device advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • device model, Android version, language and screen settings;
  • IP address, network connection type and mobile carrier;
  • information about ad impressions and clicks.

This data is processed by Yandex under its Privacy Policy. Your photos, camera image and files are never passed to the ad SDK.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

Analytics

The app uses no separate analytics services and does not itself send any information to the developer's servers.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children. The developer does not collect personal data, including data of children; the ad SDK processes the data described in "Ads".

Deleting data

All photos and app settings are removed together with the app: uninstall it or clear its data in Android settings. Photos saved to your phone gallery stay there until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
