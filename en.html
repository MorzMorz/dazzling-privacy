Privacy Policy

Dazzling Cam Shot app · effective October 29, 2026

In short

The app does not collect, store on servers or share any of your data with third parties. All photos are processed and stored on your device only.

Camera

Camera access is used only to take photos inside the app. The camera image is processed on the device and is never sent anywhere.

Photos

  • Photos you take or import are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Photos are copied to your phone gallery only when you ask ("Save to device") or if you turn on auto-save in settings.
  • When importing, the app reads only the photos you pick in the system photo picker.
  • The "Send" button passes a photo to the app you choose (messenger, e-mail, etc.) using standard Android sharing — Dazzling Cam Shot itself is not involved.

Internet, analytics and ads

The app uses no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs that collect data, and it does not send any information to any server.

Children

The app does not collect personal data, including data of children.

Deleting data

All app data is removed together with the app: uninstall it or clear its data in Android settings. Photos saved to your phone gallery stay there until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
